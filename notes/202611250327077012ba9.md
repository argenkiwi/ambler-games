In ambler-games the global `ambler` (cargo-installed) delegates to the project's `ambler` devDependency (node_modules/.bin/ambler -> ambler/cli.ts). At the repo root that delegation printed nothing and exited 0 for `ambler node`, `ambler walk` and `ambler check`, writing no files. Running `bun node_modules/.bin/ambler <verb>` works and prints what it creates. In a fresh `ambler new --target ts` folder without node_modules the global falls back to bunx and works. If a verb says nothing, assume it did nothing and use the local CLI.

Reported upstream: https://github.com/argenkiwi/ambler-ts/issues/28 (reproduced in a fresh `ambler new --target ts` after `bun install`).
