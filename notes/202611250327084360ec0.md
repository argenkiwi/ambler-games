The player always moves first in t3, so the board (9 cells) can only fill on the player's 5th move. The walk is therefore T3_PLAYER_PLACE -noWin-> T3_CHECK_GAME -continue-> T3_COMPUTER_MOVE -noWin-> T3_PRINT_BOARD. The pre-migration wiring checked after the computer instead; on a full board the old computer move wrote to board[NaN] and drew by accident, and the new immutable t3-computer-move throws `Cannot move: the board is full`. If a future change lets the computer start, a draw check is needed after its move too. Note also the AI prefers corners (1,7,3,9) before the centre, despite old docs saying centre first.
